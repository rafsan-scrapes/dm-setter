# Offer

## Offer Name

AI Income Accelerator

## What the Offer Is

AI Income Accelerator is a practical coaching and education program for people who want to build an AI-assisted online business or service.

The program helps members go from "I want to make money with AI" to a more concrete business setup: a target market, a simple offer, a client-acquisition process, AI-assisted workflows, sales messaging, and an execution plan.

## Core Promise

We help you build a practical AI-assisted offer and client-acquisition system instead of leaving you with a collection of AI tools and theory.

This is a process and support offer, not a guarantee of income or clients.

## What Is Included

### 1. Business Model Selection

We help the member choose a practical AI-assisted business model that fits their current skills, interests, resources, and target market.

Examples may include:

- AI-assisted lead generation.
- Content repurposing.
- AI automation services.
- AI-powered research services.
- Appointment-setting systems.
- Customer support automation.
- AI-enhanced creative or marketing services.

The exact model depends on the person's circumstances and should not be presented as guaranteed to work for everyone.

### 2. Offer Building

Members learn how to turn a vague service idea into a specific offer with:

- A defined target customer.
- A clearly described problem.
- A deliverable or process.
- A business outcome or benefit.
- Pricing logic.
- Positioning and messaging.

### 3. Client Acquisition System

Members learn practical ways to find and start conversations with potential buyers, including:

- Instagram outreach.
- Email outreach.
- Content-driven inbound leads.
- Lead lists and prospect research.
- Follow-up systems.
- Basic qualification.

### 4. AI Workflow Setup

Members learn how to use AI for repetitive work such as:

- Research.
- Lead qualification.
- Content drafting.
- Personalization.
- Follow-up preparation.
- Internal documentation.
- Basic workflow automation.

### 5. Sales Conversation Frameworks

Members receive frameworks for:

- Starting conversations naturally.
- Discovering the prospect's situation.
- Identifying pain points and desired outcomes.
- Explaining the offer.
- Responding to common objections.
- Moving qualified prospects to a call or checkout.

### 6. Coaching / Support

Depending on the package purchased, support may include group coaching, recorded lessons, templates, implementation guidance, community access, or direct feedback.

The AI DM setter should only describe support that is actually included in the customer's package. If unsure, escalate to a human.

## Who It Is For

This offer is best suited to people who:

- Want to build a service-based or education-based online business.
- Are interested in using AI as a productivity and delivery tool.
- Are prepared to learn sales and client acquisition.
- Are willing to implement rather than only consume content.

## Who It Is Not For

It is not a good fit for someone who:

- Wants completely passive income immediately.
- Expects AI to do everything for them.
- Wants guaranteed earnings.
- Refuses to do outreach, sales, or implementation.
- Is looking for investment advice or guaranteed financial returns.

## Pricing

**Default public price for DM conversations:** $997 USD.

Use the current checkout page or human-approved pricing as the source of truth if the price changes.

Do not invent discounts, payment plans, bonuses, deadlines, coupons, or special pricing.

### Payment Plan

If a payment plan is currently available, quote only the exact plan shown in the current offer materials. If the AI does not have the current payment-plan information, it should say:

"I don't want to give you an outdated number. I can have the team confirm the current payment options for you."

## Refund Policy

Refund terms must always match the current official policy. The AI should never improvise a refund promise.

Safe response when uncertain:

"The exact refund terms depend on the current offer policy. I don't want to misquote that, so I can get the team to confirm it for you."

## Bonuses

Bonuses should only be mentioned when they are currently active and documented in the official offer materials.

Never create urgency by inventing a bonus expiration date.

## What a Customer Should Expect

A realistic expectation is that the customer will leave with a clearer business model, offer, acquisition process, and AI-assisted workflow, then need to execute consistently to generate outcomes.

The program can reduce guesswork and provide structure, but it cannot control the market response, the person's effort, their communication skills, or whether prospects buy.

## Main CTA

For a qualified Instagram DM lead, the preferred next step is:

"The easiest next step is a quick call so we can see what you're trying to build and whether the program actually makes sense for you. Want me to send you the booking link?"

If the funnel currently uses a free training, application, or checkout page instead of a call, use that current CTA instead.

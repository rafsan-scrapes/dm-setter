# Frequently Asked Questions

## What do you actually teach?

We teach people how to use AI to build practical online income systems, especially AI-assisted services, client acquisition workflows, sales systems, and automations.

## Is this just a course about ChatGPT?

No. AI tools are only part of it. The focus is on choosing a business model, building an offer, finding potential customers, starting sales conversations, and using AI to make the process more efficient.

## Do I need to be technical?

No advanced coding background is required for the core material. Some AI workflows can become more technical depending on what you build, but the program is designed to be accessible to beginners.

## Do I need a large Instagram following?

No. A large audience is not required to start. Client acquisition can involve outbound conversations, email, niche communities, referrals, and other channels.

## Do I need an existing business?

No. Beginners can start from the business-model selection stage.

## Do I need a lot of money to get started?

The program itself has a cost, and some business models may have additional software or operating costs. We teach approaches that can start relatively lean, but we do not claim that someone can build a business with zero expenses in every case.

## How much is the program?

The default public price is **$997 USD**. The current checkout page is the source of truth for any active payment options or promotions.

## Is there a payment plan?

There may be payment options depending on the current offer. If the AI does not have the latest terms, it should route the prospect to a human rather than guessing.

## Is there a refund?

Refund terms depend on the current official policy. The AI should never promise a refund unless the exact policy is available.

## How long does it take to make money?

There is no guaranteed timeline. Some people may get traction faster than others. Outcomes depend on the business model, offer, market, execution, sales ability, consistency, and other factors.

## How much money can I make?

There is no guaranteed income amount. The purpose of the program is to help you build a system capable of generating revenue, but actual results vary from person to person.

## Can AI make money for me automatically?

Not by itself. AI can automate or accelerate parts of research, delivery, content, follow-up, and operations, but a real business still needs a valuable offer, customers, execution, and human judgment.

## Can I do this part-time?

Yes, many workflows can be built and operated part-time, but the required time depends on the business model and the person's goals. Starting part-time does not guarantee a particular outcome.

## How many hours per day do I need?

There is no universal number. A beginner building a service business typically needs regular time for learning, prospecting, sales conversations, and delivery. Consistency matters more than a specific hourly promise.

## What business model should I choose?

That depends on your skills, experience, interests, available time, preferred customer type, and how you want to sell. We can help narrow it down instead of forcing everyone into the same model.

## Can you choose a business model for me?

We can help you evaluate suitable models based on your situation, but there is no single model that guarantees success for everyone.

## Do you provide clients?

The program teaches client-acquisition methods and systems. It should not be described as a guaranteed-client service unless a specific package explicitly includes that service.

## Do you write my outreach messages for me?

Members can use provided frameworks, templates, and AI workflows to create and personalize outreach. The exact level of done-for-you support depends on the package.

## Do you help with closing sales?

Yes, the training includes sales-conversation frameworks and objection handling. The goal is to help members understand and manage sales conversations rather than promise that every prospect will close.

## Is this an agency opportunity?

The program can be used to build an AI-assisted service business, including an agency-style model, but members choose the model that fits them.

## Can I use Instagram only?

Yes, Instagram can be one acquisition channel. We also discuss other channels because relying on a single platform can limit opportunities.

## Do I need a website?

Not necessarily at the beginning. The minimum setup depends on the offer and acquisition channel. A simple profile, clear offer, proof, and a way to book or pay may be enough to start some models.

## What tools do I need?

The exact stack varies by business model. Typical tools may include an AI assistant, spreadsheet or CRM, communication tools, and automation software. We prioritize simple tools and avoid adding software without a reason.

## Do you teach automation?

Yes. Automation is included where it helps reduce repetitive work, follow-up, research, or administration.

## Do I get direct access to you?

Only promise the support level that is included in the customer's purchased package. Do not imply unlimited personal access if that is not part of the offer.

## Is this beginner friendly?

Yes. Beginners can start with the fundamentals. Existing freelancers and creators can also adapt the material to an existing skill or audience.

## Can someone outside the US join?

Yes, the core training is online. Payment availability and local business considerations can vary by country.

## Is this legitimate?

The program is designed around real business activities such as selling services, acquiring clients, improving delivery, and using AI for productivity. It is not presented as guaranteed or risk-free income.

## What happens after I join?

You start with the business model and offer foundation, then move into acquisition, sales, AI workflow setup, and implementation according to the program structure.

## Can I get the information first?

Yes. A prospect can ask questions before deciding. The goal is to determine whether the offer fits rather than pressure them into buying.

## Can I talk to someone before buying?

For qualified prospects, the normal next step is a short call with the team to understand their situation and answer remaining questions.

## Where do I start?

Start by describing what you are trying to accomplish, your current skill level, and whether you already have an audience, service, or business. We can then point you toward the most relevant next step.
